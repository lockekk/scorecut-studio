# Privacy Policy — ScoreCut Studio

**Last updated: 2026-05-10**

## Summary

ScoreCut Studio runs entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on our servers. There is no account system, no analytics, and no advertising in this version.

## Who we are

ScoreCut Studio is developed by Locke Huang, an individual based in New Zealand.

Contact: **scorecutstudio@gmail.com**

## What we collect

**Nothing.** This version of ScoreCut Studio does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information with us or any third party.

All of the following stays on your device:

- Sheet music images, video files, and audio files you import or capture
- OCR-extracted text from your sheet music
- Project files, edit history, and preferences
- Captured frames, exported pages, and saved PDFs

## Permissions

ScoreCut Studio requests the following permissions, used solely for the features described:

- **Camera** — to capture sheet-music pages and to scan license QR codes from the desktop app. Camera frames are processed on-device only.
- **Photo Library** — to let you import sheet-music images and select QR-code images you have saved. Read-only; we do not write back to your photo library.
- **Local Network** — to let the desktop app communicate with your phone during license pairing. No data leaves your local network.
- **Files** — to let you choose where to save exported pages and to import files you select. We only access files you explicitly pick.
- **Microphone** — *not used.* The audio features in ScoreCut Studio process audio extracted from video files you provide; the microphone is never recorded.

## Where data is stored

All data stays in storage your operating system manages for the app — either inside the app's private container, or in folders you explicitly choose using the file picker. Nothing is uploaded.

## Third-party services

This version of ScoreCut Studio uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Children's privacy

ScoreCut Studio does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. The app is suitable for all ages and does not contain advertising or in-app purchases in this version.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top will change. For material changes, the in-app and App Store listings will reference the updated policy.

## Your rights

If you have questions about this policy or want to contact us about your data, email **scorecutstudio@gmail.com**. Because we collect no data in this version, there is nothing on our end to retrieve, export, or delete. Local app data on your device can be removed by deleting the app from your device.
